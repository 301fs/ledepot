# Target Niches — NYC Tri-State (NY / NJ / CT)

Ranked by fit for a website-first, done-for-you tech offer to non-technical owners. "Fit" = low existing tech presence × proven ability to pay × owner is busy doing the work (not at a computer). Start at the top.

## Tier 1 — best fit (busy owners, real money, often weak web presence)

**Home services / trades** — HVAC, plumbing, electricians, roofing, landscaping/lawn care, pest control, paving/masonry, garage doors, fencing. Owners are in the field all day; many run on a phone number and word of mouth. High job values = budget. Demo hook: a clean site with a "request a quote" form and click-to-call.
*Queries:* `plumber in Paramus NJ`, `HVAC in New Rochelle NY`, `roofing contractor in Danbury CT`.

**Auto** — independent repair shops, body shops, transmission specialists, detailers, tire shops. Loyal repeat customers, steady cash flow, frequently no site or a dead one. Demo hook: online appointment request + services/pricing.
*Queries:* `auto repair in Yonkers NY`, `auto body shop in Elizabeth NJ`, `car detailing in Stamford CT`.

**Health & wellness practices** — dentists, chiropractors, physical therapy, optometrists, med spas, dermatology, audiology, podiatry. Higher-ticket, recurring patients, and many still book by phone. Med spas especially have money and image-consciousness. Demo hook: online booking + before/after gallery.
*Queries:* `chiropractor in Hoboken NJ`, `med spa in White Plains NY`, `physical therapy in Greenwich CT`.

## Tier 2 — strong fit

**Food** — independent restaurants, caterers, bakeries, delis, pizzerias, food trucks. Often social-only or stuck on a marketplace; many want their own online ordering to escape delivery-app fees. Demo hook: own-branded menu + online ordering/reservations.
*Queries:* `caterer in Montclair NJ`, `italian restaurant in Scarsdale NY`, `bakery in Fairfield CT`.

**Personal care** — hair salons, barbershops, nail salons, day spas, lash/brow studios. Appointment-driven, image-driven, frequently Instagram-only. Demo hook: online booking + service menu.
*Queries:* `hair salon in Jersey City NJ`, `barbershop in New Rochelle NY`, `nail salon in Norwalk CT`.

**Professional services** — small law offices, accountants/CPAs, insurance brokers, real estate teams, financial advisors. Money is there; sites are often dated and templated. Demo hook: credibility site + lead/intake form + automation upsell.
*Queries:* `law office in Newark NJ`, `accountant in Mount Vernon NY`, `insurance agency in Bridgeport CT`.

## Tier 3 — opportunistic

**Specialty retail & local services** — florists, furniture/mattress, jewelers, pet groomers/boarding, dance/martial-arts/fitness studios, photographers, event/wedding vendors, cleaning services, moving companies. Mixed tech maturity — qualify case by case; the scoring script will sort the good ones out.

---

## Notes for picking a batch

- **Run one niche per batch.** A demo and pitch tuned to "plumbers" converts better than a generic one, and you reuse the same demo template across the batch.
- **Higher-ticket niches forgive weaker volume.** A med spa or law office with 20 reviews can still spend more than a deli with 200.
- **"Social-only" is a goldmine** in food and personal care — they already accept they need an online presence, they just outsourced it to Instagram. Easy yes to "here's your own site."
- **Geography:** spread queries across NJ (Bergen, Hudson, Essex, Union), NY (Westchester, lower Hudson Valley, parts of NYC outer boroughs), and CT (Fairfield County) for volume. Fairfield County CT and Westchester NY skew higher-income.
